\chapter{Plan} 
\label{cha:plan}

The main points that have been followed to reach the final design are:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Having too many signals make debugging extremely slow and burdensome, so I would change those into a compact and reusable interface.
    \item Introducing a clocking block will surely make the timing management more easy and less prone to errors.
    \item The transaction has too many not needed signals and wraps both masters. I will separate the masters and use strictly useful signals, leaving simulation control to UVM.
    \item Instead of a long and repetitive generator, I will make simple building blocks that can be reused across tests and make the creation of new test fast and easy.
    \item After making the timing management automatic, dividing the agents into two separate ones should be easy and make the structure extremely scalable.
    \item I will introduce the concept of coverage and hardware checking.
\end{enumerate}
